WELCOME TO MY MAP PAGE

Jones College Prep

Located in the middle of downtown, Jones College Prep, a CPS selective enrollment school, often gets mistaken as a corporate office, although on the inside, the lockers resemble more of a school than work space. I love the proximity of my school to downtown and the access to opportunities I have here. I've made amazing friends and had incredible teachers. The classes I've enjoyed most were AP Calculus BC, AP US Government, and AP CSA.

Hexe Coffee Shop

About half a mile from my house, Hexe coffee shop is located on the busy corner of Clybourn, Damen, and Diversey. The coffee shop has a spooky vibe with an all black interior, but the open windows and warm lighting somehow fight off the erieness and convert those feelings to comfort. I love the coffee here, but most of all, I enjoy the egg and cheese biscuit sandwich; I highly recommend!

The Summer Hangout Spot

Over the Summer, the cement ledge on Belmont and Lakeshore drive, becomes a hotspot for hangouts. I enjoy swimming in the lake to cool off on a hot Summer day, going on long runs along the lakefront, and watching the fireworks at Navy Pier with my best friends while eating a box of sushi with drinks!
